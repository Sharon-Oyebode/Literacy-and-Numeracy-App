How to use:
1. Navigate the menu and enter the number for the menu option of your choice 
Press 1 for Literacy Lessons
Press 2 for Numeracy Lessons 
Press 3 for Quizzes
Press 4 to view Progress
Press 5 to Exit

2. LITERACY LESSONS
In this module, you learn the alphabets, simple words and simple sentences. Follow the on screen instructions.

3. NUMERACY LESSONS
In this module, you learn numbers, basic math operations like addition, subtraction, multiplication and division

4. QUIZ
Your knowledge is tested on your literacy and numeracy skills
Follow on screen instruction to submit answers 

5. VIEW PROGRESS 
Check the lessons you have completed and view your quiz scores.

Other tips:
If you make a mistake, the program will guide you back to try again.

Enjoy Learning!
